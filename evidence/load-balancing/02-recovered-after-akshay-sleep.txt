=== Load balancing recovered after troubleshooting ===

Issue found: Akshay's laptop had gone to sleep, so Backend B (10.7.1.139:3002) stopped
responding -- this caused nginx requests round-robined to B to hang/timeout, while requests
to A (Hemanth, still awake) succeeded. This is a real-world illustration of why HA/failover
matters (Phase 2 territory), and also shows nginx's default behavior does NOT automatically
skip a hung (not-yet-erroring) backend the way it does a connection-refused one.

Fix: Akshay's laptop woken up, backend B restarted.
