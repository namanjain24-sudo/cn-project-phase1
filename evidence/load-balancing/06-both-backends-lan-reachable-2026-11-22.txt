Both answer from a third machine, which proves each server.js is bound to
0.0.0.0 and not 127.0.0.1. If they were bound to loopback they would work
locally and nginx on Mac 2 could never reach them -- a 502 whose cause is
invisible from the edge.

Each response carries its own X-Backend header, which is what makes the
load balancing in Task D observable later.

Ports in use: Backend A :3001, Backend B :3002, as fixed in the spec.
