The spec awards marks for METHODOLOGY. scripts/diagnose.sh walks the layers
in the order the spec names -- name resolution, then TCP, then TLS, then the
application -- and stops at the LOWEST broken layer, because nothing above a
broken layer means anything.

WHAT TO SAY WHILE THIS RUNS DURING THE FAULT INJECTION
  "Name resolution first -- does the name become an IP, and is it the RIGHT
   IP? Then transport -- is that IP reachable and is the port open? Refused
   means nothing is listening; timeout means something is filtering us. Then
   TLS -- does the certificate validate without bypassing it? Then the edge
   itself: any HTTP response at all, even a 502, is generated BY nginx, so
   receiving one proves the edge is alive. Then the application."
